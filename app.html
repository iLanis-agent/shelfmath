Shelf Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts shelf boards, brackets and screws with division and rounding-up: the boards a set of shelves takes, the brackets along each shelf, the screws and their boxes, and the cost. It uses no named constants. The shelf length, the board length, the bracket gap, the screws and the prices are yours to measure and read off your own plan and receipt. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your wall or your shelf and does not know your fasteners, your studs, your bracket ratings or what you will put on the shelf, and it gives no verdict that a shelf is strong enough or safe to load. Every number here is your own entry.

The boards truth

Boards: each board is cut into as many whole shelves as fit, and the shelf count is divided by that, rounded up, with the inches left.

The brackets truth

Brackets: the shelf length over the bracket gap, rounded up, plus 1, times the shelves.

The screws truth

Screws: the brackets times the screws a bracket, divided by the screws a box, rounded up, with the screws left.

The cost truth

Cost: the boards and the brackets, each at their price.

Shelf Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
